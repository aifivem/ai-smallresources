# ai-smallresources

Small gameplay features, each behind a panel switch: seatbelt, cruise control, no seat shuffle and a seat swap key, vehicle push, flipping overturned vehicles, vehicle radio off, fake plates, boat ropes, crouch, finger pointing, hands up, falling over (ragdoll), sitting on objects, carrying players, tackle, anti ghost-peek, binoculars, firework, news gear, notepad and dive gear items, AFK kick, no GTA dispatch / wanted level, no GTA police / emergency / army spawns (with panel areas where vehicle generators stay off), no ambient planes, no police radio, quiet city ambience, no garbage trucks, no idle camera, hidden GTA HUD parts and ammo count, ambient population with density zones and an admin NPC switch, areas without GTA vehicles / scenario people / any people, map blips, a vehicle camera (close zoom while aiming, first / third person only), driving tweaks (falling out of a fast rear seat, no passenger weight, no double clutch, full driving skill), weapon rules (recoil per group or weapon, drive-by limits, aim to fire, no blind fire from cover, no combat roll, A / D macro breaker, player melee damage), ledge grab, `/unstuck` right after joining, game-feel tweaks (no combat stance after a fight, no pistol whip, no blind peeking from cover, optional no one shot headshots, no bunny hopping, no GTA pickups, no weapons from vehicles, no GTA stealth walk, sprint indoors, no automatic helmet, hats stay on, no mouse steering, no steering in the air, open hood and trunk breaking off, NPC melee damage), teleports between two points, elevators with floors and Discord Rich Presence. Depends on ai-core (prompts, progress, menus, jobs); uses the active inventory through ai-core's inventory layer (lib `inventory`) for the items.

## Decisions

- Windscreen ejection is GTA's own (`SetFlyThroughWindscreenParams`), only the speeds change with the belt.
- The seat belt reaches HUDs as the client local event `SEATBELT_EVENT` (boolean while the feature is on, null otherwise) and the client export `GetSeatbelt()` for late starters; ai-hud shows its belt warning from it. The exit key is blocked through lib `controls` while buckled.
- Every per-frame job (blocking keys, hiding HUD parts, the binocular camera, pointing, density multipliers) is a `TickLoop` that runs only while needed; anti ghost-peek checks every 50 ms only while armed.
- GTA world tweaks (`client/world.ts`) are global natives set once per settings change. Emergency spawns: random cops off, emergency vehicle scenario types off, emergency / army vehicle and ped models suppressed (population only; script-created entities are not affected). The panel areas (`emergencyAreas`, defaults from the qbx_smallresources lists plus the prison) turn GTA's vehicle generators off in a box around each point: a 5 s job (only while areas exist) does it again with `RemoveVehiclesFromGeneratorsInArea` and the global calls whenever the player comes within radius + 400 m, because generators come back when their map part streams in again. Default radii are smaller than the reference scripts' 300-500 m (generators of every model go off inside, so a big box empties the city's parked cars).
- Open hood / trunk break off (`client/doors.ts`, from TGIANN's crew server): a 100 ms job only while the player drives tears an open hood or trunk off (`SetVehicleDoorBroken`, the door falls as an object) above `breakDoorsSpeed` km/h or below 900 body health; the driver's client owns the vehicle, so the broken door reaches everyone. NPC melee damage (`client/weapons.ts`): `SetAiMeleeWeaponDamageModifier(npcMeleeDamage / 100)` once per settings change (fists, bats, knives together; default 35 %, crew's value), `ResetAiMeleeWeaponDamageModifier` while off and when the resource stops.
- Combat stance (`client/stance.ts`): GTA has no flag against action mode, so a 100 ms job on foot (only while the setting or anti bunny hop is on) switches it off with `SetPedUsingActionMode(ped, false, -1, "DEFAULT_ACTION")` unless the ped aims (config flag 78). Anti bunny hop: a second jump within 5 s trips the player 600 ms later (not while climbing, falling or parachuting).
- Vehicle weapon rewards are blocked per frame (`DisablePlayerVehicleRewards` holds one frame) only for 5 s after each seat change. Pistol whip, stealth (duck control 36 on foot) and air control (59 / 60 while a land vehicle's wheels are all off the ground, checked every 100 ms by the driver) go through lib `controls`. Crouch never toggles while the ped is see-through or invisible (admin noclip, same key).
- Gestures (`client/gestures/`): pointing stops by itself when the player aims, falls, fights or ragdolls; hands up (`random@mugging3`, also in vehicles) never starts over another script's animation (tasks 134 / 135 / 118) and ends when its animation is replaced, the player falls or dies; ragdoll lasts while its key is held.
- Firework placing, plate work and boat ropes run on the server with lib `progressFor` (firework 3 s, plate 6 s counted after 90 %, facing the vehicle; rope 3 s facing the boat, checked again after it); the item is taken and the plate swapped only if the player is still there afterwards (`server/items.ts`, `server/vehicles.ts`). Writing a note is a dialog, not a timed bar: a lib `PendingActions` token with `minRatio: 0` that the note text comes back with.
- Fireworks: the server fans the spot out to players within 400 m (lib `playersNear`, scope candidates); effects are local per client.
- News gear: the server toggles the player state `aiHold` (item name or false); every client attaches a local prop to that player's ped (`resource/client/news.ts`).
- Carry: one action picks the nearest free player up or puts them down. A downed or cuffed player is picked up at once, anyone else answers a menu first (10 s); only a player who agreed can get down on their own. The pair lives in two player states, each client plays its own part (`client/carry.ts`); leaving (drop, logout) ends the pair (`onCharacter` `unloaded`). After a declined ask the carrier waits 30 s before asking again (lib `Cooldowns`). The action is a lib `radial` item (whichever radial menu runs) shown while the setting is on (`canShow`).
- Fake plate: the real plate is kept in the vehicle state `aiRealPlate`; ai-vehicles saves owned vehicles with their real plate anyway. Fitting needs the vehicle's keys; the key holder gets keys for the plate the vehicle shows.
- Boat rope: a boat within 12 m of a panel mooring is frozen on the server and marked `aiMoored`; the boats are every class 14 model, found as vehicle model points. A boat another player drives is never tied or freed.
- Seats are panel records (one model each, seat offsets relative to the object, a seat type = scenario or animation in `common/seats.ts`); seeded from a community sit list's vanilla and prop models. The nearest free seat of the object is taken; the getting-up prompt is ai-core's prompt.
- Teleports and elevators: the server checks the end / floor the player stands at (and access: a teleport's lib access fields, `online.allows`; an elevator floor's `job` text, read into an access rule because list rows have no job / gang pickers) and answers the target; the client teleports with lib `player.teleport` behind a fade (collision loaded). Teleport and elevator points come from `RecordObjects` (one record's points per edit); a teleport's points show only to who passes its access rule.
- Seats stay one model point over every seat model (364 seeded): one model point per record would query GTA per model, so a seat edit re-adds that one point. Moorings are no world objects (the two boat model points read them through `nearestOf`), so a mooring edit only re-adds those two points.
- Binoculars and the news camera hide the HUD (lib `hud`) and show their zoom and close keybinds as key hints; zoom is two keybinds on the mouse wheel. Pointing's "blocked" probe reads the previous frame's async shape test.
- Dive gear (port of qbx_divegear, its timings: 5 s on / off / refill, 100 s of air): `diving_gear` toggles the gear through lib `progressFor`, `diving_fill` refills the tank (taken after the bar). Air is seconds per character in ai-core's metadata `diveOxygen` (a new character starts empty, as in Qbox) and is spent only underwater, counted on the wearer's client (one per second, `every(1000)`), which reports it every 10 s and at 0; the server only lowers the stored value. While worn and with air the client sets `SetEnableScuba` and a long breath time; empty or taken off gives GTA's breath back (1 s empty, 50 s off, as Qbox). The "swimming / underwater" checks before wearing and refilling are the client's answer to the `diveWater` server callback. Props: the server sets the player state `aiDiving`, every client attaches a local tank and mask (like news gear). Taking the gear item away while worn does not take the gear off (as Qbox). A restart or character switch takes it off.
- Discord Rich Presence (`client/discord.ts`, panel file `discord`, tab Discord): the natives are set on each panel change and the text (two lines; FiveM sends the part before `\n` as Discord's second line `state`, the rest as the first line `details`) only when an input it shows changed: player count from the GlobalState keys `aiPlayerCount` / `aiMaxPlayers` (server sets them while the feature is on, joins and leaves of 5 s as one set), character and job from lib player data (job label from ai-core's client `GetDefinitions`, read once per job), street every 15 s only while a line uses `{street}`. FiveM keeps the elapsed timer from the connection; a removed button is cleared with an empty label (the native skips empty labels); switching off gives FiveM's own presence back only if this resource set it (null to the natives), so another presence script is never overwritten.
- Newer features (`common/features.ts`, sections in `server/schema/featureSections.ts`, cards in `featureCards.ts`) and the `weapons` panel file (Weapons tab) take their behaviour and numbers from qbx_smallresources on TGIANN's crew server (`noshuff`, `vehiclepush`, `flipvehicle`, `tgiann`, `betterCam`, `tgiann_weapons`, `melee_damage`, `tgiannVehiclesync`, `blips`, `climp`, `tgi_kurtul`).
- Seat swap (`client/shuffle.ts`, key O): `TaskShuffleToNextVehicleSeat` between the front seats only, refused while cuffed (`PLAYER_BAGS.cuffed`) or wearing this resource's belt.
- Vehicle push (`client/push.ts` pusher, `client/pushOwner.ts` owner, `server/pushFlip.ts`): E + Shift (two keybinds) next to a vehicle without a driver whose engine is at or below `pushEngineHealth` (default 1000 = any, crew's value) or whose fuel (lib `fuel`) is under 3, class not in `pushBlockedClasses`. The pusher attaches and plays `missfinale_c2ig_11`; the mode (`f` / `b` + `l` / `r`) goes to the server, which writes the vehicle state `aiPush`; the client that owns the vehicle moves it every frame (`SetVehicleForwardSpeed`, `TaskVehicleTempAction` 11 / 10 for the wheels as the reference). The pusher watches the owner every 500 ms and sends the mode again on a change; the server clears and sets the same value again 100 ms later so the new owner's change handler fires. Stops on Shift release, death, ragdoll, entering a vehicle or the vehicle no longer qualifying.
- Flip (`client/flip.ts`): a `points.addVehicle` option with `preferTarget` while the roll is 70 degrees or more; the server runs `progressFor` (`flipSeconds`, default 15), checks distance and tilt again (pitch or roll: the server's rotation order differs from the client's) and sends `flipped` to `NetworkGetEntityOwner` (the player when nobody owns it), whose client calls `SetVehicleOnGroundProperly`.
- Vehicle tweaks (`client/vehicleTweaks.ts`): leaving a rear seat (index 1 and up) above `rearRagdollSpeed` m/s (not GTA's jump out) gives the ped the vehicle's velocity and ragdolls it 5 s; `SetGlobalPassengerMassMultiplier(0)`; anti double clutch every 500 ms while driving (gear under 3 at full rpm above 50 mph pulls the rpm to 0.3 each frame until it drops under 0.6, then an 800 ms pause); `MP0_WHEELIE_ABILITY` / `MP0_DRIVING_ABILITY` 100 (set when on, never reset).
- Vehicle camera (`client/camera.ts`): GTA's next-camera control (0) is disabled through lib `controls` while on and the keybind V toggles first / third person for the ped and vehicle cameras, so the middle view never comes; aiming in a vehicle sets follow zoom 4 and puts the old zoom back (100 ms job only in a vehicle).
- Recoil (`client/recoil.ts`): lib `onShot` lifts the camera by the weapon's own row or its group's (`GetWeapontypeGroup(hash) >>> 0`: the native can answer signed, the panel stores unsigned); any inventory, hash only. Player melee damage: `SetWeaponDamageModifier(hash, percent / 100)` on the listed weapons (default 25 %), back to 1 when off, removed from the list or on stop. NPC melee damage stays `npcMeleeDamage`.
- Drive-by (`client/driveBy.ts`, 100 ms job only in a vehicle) owns `SetPlayerCanDoDriveBy`: blocked above `driveBySpeed` m/s while the player state `instance` (routing bucket, kept by ai-core's QB bridge on every bucket change) is 0, from the front seats of `noAimModels`, looking back (a point 40 m behind on screen) in `noLookBackModels`, and through an open unbroken hood (looking forward) or trunk (looking back).
- Firing rules (`client/fireRules.ts`): in cover without aiming or ghosted (100 ms check with a weapon) `DisablePlayerFiring` every frame; on foot one frame job reads the aim key (25, `IsDisabledControlPressed`): without it no firing and melee keys 140-142 off (`aimToFire`, fists too, as the reference); with a weapon aimed jump (22) off; more than `strafeTaps` A / D presses in a second while aiming force control 30 for `strafeBreakMs`. The frame job runs on foot whenever `aimToFire` is on.
- Density (`client/density.ts`): the Population card's multipliers, replaced inside a `densityZones` record (lib `Zones` per record through `RecordObjects`, the last entered wins; traffic and pedestrians each optional), pedestrians 0 within radius + 100 m of a `noPedZones` area (cleared once on entering), everything but parked cars 0 while the GlobalState key `aiNpcDisabled` is set. A forced 0 from a zone or the NPC switch clears 100 m around the player every second (generators for traffic, `ClearAreaOfPeds` for people); the card's own 0 does not.
- Population areas (`client/areas.ts`, `client/nearAreas.ts`): `blockVehicleAreas` (default none), `blockScenarioAreas` (default the six Fleeca counters, 12 m) and `noPedZones` (default none) get a scenario blocking area once per change of the lists (removed on a change and on stop); vehicle areas turn generators off and ped areas clear their peds each time the player comes within radius + 400 / + 100 m (`NearAreas`, the emergency areas' 5 s job).
- Map blips: the `blips` records file through `RecordObjects` and lib `Blips`, all hidden by `mapBlips`.
- Ledge grab (`client/ledge.ts`): a 250 ms check turns a frame probe on only while airborne and allowed (not in a vehicle, down, cuffed, ragdolled, swimming, climbing, vaulting, parachuting, see-through or invisible = noclip); three `raycast`s (wall at `armReach`, ledge top between `topMin` and `topMax`, 1.6 m headroom), then the velocity assist and `TaskClimb`, with the reference's timeouts and cooldowns. The export `SetLedgeGrabEnabled(false)` is a lib `Reasons` entry of the calling resource (gone when it stops).
- `/unstuck` (`server/unstuck.ts`): a window of `unstuckMinutes` (default 2) opens at each character load (characters replayed after a restart get none) with a notification; the server asks the client (`unstuckCheck`: inside an interior or more than 2 m under the ground) and runs a cancelable `progressFor` (`unstuckSeconds`, default 300), then closes the window and sends the panel point; the client teleports with `player.teleport`. One success per load; a cancel can retry inside the window.
- Notes: the notepad item stays; a note item carries `text` and a `description` (the inventory tooltip shows it) in its metadata; reading uses ai-core's input dialog with a read-only multiline field.

## Contracts

- Events and callbacks: `resource/common/small.ts`. Client local event `SEATBELT_EVENT` and export `GetSeatbelt()`; client exports `IsHandsUp()`, `IsPointing()`, `SetLedgeGrabEnabled(enabled)`; server exports `SetNpcDisabled(on: boolean)` (GlobalState `aiNpcDisabled`; ai-admin's `/npc` calls it) and `IsNpcDisabled()` (typed `AiSmallResourcesServerExports` in `@aifivem/shared`); command `/unstuck`; replicated player states `aiHold`, `aiCarrying`, `aiCarriedBy`, `aiDiving`; character metadata `diveOxygen`; vehicle states `aiRealPlate`, `aiMoored`, `aiPush` (`PushMode`); reads the player state `instance` (routing bucket, ai-core bridge).
- Items: `binoculars`, `firework1`-`firework4`, `newscam`, `newsmic`, `newsboom`, `fakeplate`, `notepad`, `note` (ai-inventory `data/items.json`), `diving_gear`, `diving_fill` (Qbox names; must exist in the active inventory).
- Panel files: `settings`, `weapons`, `teleports`, `elevators`, `moorings`, `seats`, `densityZones`, `blips`, `discord` (all `sync: "all"`).

## Entry points

- Server: panel content `resource/server/content.ts`, schema `server/schema/` (one file per panel section), tackle and AFK `player.ts`, teleports and elevators `places.ts`, items `items.ts`, dive gear `dive.ts`, fake plate and boat rope `vehicles.ts`, carry `carry.ts`, vehicle push and flip `pushFlip.ts`, NPC switch exports `npc.ts`, `/unstuck` `unstuck.ts`.
- Client: one module per feature in `resource/client/`; panel files `client/settings.ts`.
